This page explains how CareerPrepBook collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use our website and app.

Information we collect

  • Account details you provide (for example: name, mobile number, email, login credentials).
  • Learning content you create or submit (words, meanings, interview Q&A, quiz attempts, notes).
  • Usage and device data needed to run the app (pages visited, feature usage, basic diagnostics).
  • Cookies/local storage used for core functionality such as keeping you signed in and remembering preferences.

How we use information

  • To provide core features (Word List, Interview Q&A, Quiz, Output Practice, progress tracking).
  • To personalize your experience (showing your contributions and progress where applicable).
  • To communicate with you about support requests and important service updates.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • To improve the product by understanding which features are useful and where users get stuck.

Advertising and third-party services

CareerPrepBook may display ads. Advertising providers (for example, Google AdSense) may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure ads, including interest-based ads depending on your browser settings and region. CareerPrepBook does not control third-party cookies.

How we share information

  • With service providers that help us operate the app (hosting, infrastructure, analytics, email/support tooling).
  • For legal and safety reasons if required by law or to protect users, the public, or the service.
  • With your direction (for example, if you choose to publish content or share it within the app).

Data retention

We keep data for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our terms. We also may retain some information in backups for a limited period.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

  • You can review and update your profile information from the Profile page (when available).
  • You can request account deletion by contacting support. We may ask you to verify your identity.
  • You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect app functionality.

Children’s privacy

CareerPrepBook is intended for general audiences preparing for careers. If you believe a minor has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.
